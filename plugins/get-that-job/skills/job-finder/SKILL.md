# JobFinder

Use when the applicant asks to find, continue searching, assess, or prepare job opportunities. Check the workspace setup marker; if absent, use [SetupSkill](../setup-skill/SKILL.md). If it says `intake_gate: awaiting-documents`, wait for the explicit first-use continue or skip before reading CVs or starting the pipeline. Read `AGENTS.md`, `MEMORY.md`, `APPLICATION_PROFILE.md`, `TARGET_ROLES.md`, `OPERATIONS.md`, `CV_INDEX.md`, `QUALIFICATIONS_INDEX.md`, `CoverLetters/README.md`, `APPLICATIONS.csv`, and `SEARCH_LOG.md`. If the source CV or a decisive search preference is missing after the handoff, use [SetupChecker](../setup-checker/SKILL.md) and [ProfileIntake](../profile-intake/SKILL.md) while continuing independent work.

## Search through checked draft

1. Search LinkedIn Jobs and suitable employer or job sites for **every role or field the applicant explicitly requested**, plus the confirmed or delegated source-based suggestions in `TARGET_ROLES.md`, within their location, pay, and employment-type constraints. Use CV and optional applicant LinkedIn profile evidence to suggest adjacent roles and assess fit; LinkedIn Jobs listings are a vacancy source, not the applicant's profile. Do not assume every past role is desired, and do not omit a user-stated target because the CV or profile lacks a matching title. If priorities are missing, use [ProfileIntake](../profile-intake/SKILL.md) to obtain them or, when the applicant expressly delegated target selection, label an initial source-based search provisional and surface its assumptions. Search in the user's preferred location order. Prefer the live employer posting over snippets, repost labels, or aggregators. Exclude stale, duplicate, fee-charging, and unverifiable listings. Apply the user's paid/unpaid and employment-type rules; verify that a conditional stipend would actually be available to this applicant. Verify employer, requisition, duties, essential criteria, deadline and time zone, compensation, nationality/work authorization, remote or location rules, requested documents, and application route. Do not inherit the plugin creator's job targets or nationality assumptions.
2. Check the tracker, search log, and accessible signed-in application histories **before** tailoring or opening a new packet. Do not resurface an opportunity the applicant declined unless they ask to reconsider it or material facts change. Compare every essential requirement against approved CVs and official credential evidence. Mark requirements met, unclear, or unmet, with the source. Treat nationality-only categories, licensing, degree, location, and work authorization as hard conditions. Stop on an unmet mandatory condition unless the employer explicitly accepts an alternative. Log rejected or uncertain roles with the reason.
3. For a strong eligible role, create `applications/<ID>/` with a packet README following `applications/README.md`. Update the tracker and search log with the posting URL, source, fit, and stage. Create **both** a tailored CV and a cover letter, plus requested extras. Use the closest approved CV as a factual starting point and incorporate supported facts from other approved CVs as relevant; resolve conflicts using direct corrections and official evidence. Separately follow the applicant's chosen CV visual design reference in `CV_INDEX.md`. For the letter, use the bundled generic style DOCX in `CoverLetters/Template/` by default, or the applicant's own approved reference if they selected one in `CoverLetters/README.md`. Replace every placeholder with verified role-specific information and remove unused fields. If several approved CVs have different designs and no reference is chosen, ask the applicant before generating the tailored CV while continuing other independent work. Preserve factual labels for employment, internships, volunteer work, observerships, research, and personal projects. Follow the employer's output format. Pair every new Word application document with a same-stem PDF in the packet; use the PDF for review and upload unless the employer requires another format.
4. Check every visible page, extracted text, role and employer names, links, document title/subject and other properties, and embedded comments before sharing or uploading. Remove generator traces and unrelated inherited metadata. If a portal accepts a plain-text letter, prepare a separate version that begins with the salutation, uses readable paragraphs and a normal closing, omits the formatted letter's contact header/date/recipient/subject/footer, and fits the character limit.
5. A request to search using this workflow authorizes preparation and filling a reversible portal draft when accessible, but does not select every match for final submission. Check `APPLICATION_PROFILE.md` before asking for recurring fields, and reuse only answers that remain valid for this form and scope. Enter all supported personal, work, education, reference, eligibility, disclosure, document, and other application data. Check permission to contact each reference for the particular application. Ask about unknown mandatory facts or commitments. Compare entered names, dates, credential status, nationality, compensation, and files with the sources and live posting. Save and reopen or inspect the persisted draft and uploads. Record every unanswered field. If authentication, a broken control, or an unknown requirement blocks progress, finish independent preparation and state the exact blocker.
6. After each filled and checked draft or material draft update, invoke [ProfileBuilder](../profile-builder/SKILL.md) to capture newly verified reusable answers before handoff.

## LinkedIn access

Check the selected LinkedIn app's available tools before choosing a search method. Use a job-search tool when one is actually exposed. A people-search tool cannot establish a job listing; use it only for relevant person research. For LinkedIn Jobs, use a signed-in browser when the app has no job-search tool. Confirm sign-in from the page, rather than assuming a connected app or an open tab means the user is signed in. If LinkedIn requires sign-in or access has expired, tell the user to sign in through LinkedIn's official page or connector prompt, leave credentials and verification challenges to the user, and resume LinkedIn searching afterward. Continue searching employer sites while access is pending. If access remains unavailable, identify that coverage gap in the search log and handoff; do not claim LinkedIn was searched.

## Handoff

Read both the standing submission mode and **Opportunity choice** in `OPERATIONS.md` for every application. A missing mode means `stop-and-wait`; a missing opportunity choice means no standing automatic opportunity scope. In the default submission mode, set a complete checked packet to `ready-for-review`, give the applicant the employer, role, why it fits, any concerns, packet, portal location, deadline, completed fields, questions, and final action, and stop before Apply/Submit. The applicant chooses which opportunities to submit by clicking Apply or directing Codex to submit a specific application. Record declined roles and the reason in the search log and relevant packet or tracker row, without treating a decline as a new global exclusion unless the applicant says so. In `automatic-submit` mode, pass a checked application to [ApplicationFinalize](../application-finalize/SKILL.md) only when the applicant explicitly selected that specific employer and role or the role fits an explicitly recorded standing automatic opportunity scope. Record the selection or scope match and its source/date in the packet and tracker notes. Search targets or a generic "find me jobs" request alone do not establish that choice. If no choice covers the role, present it for selection and stop before Apply/Submit; continue other useful search and draft work. If a required fact, consent, signature, or other blocker remains unresolved, record it and stop for the applicant. Never enter a name or mark an e-signature, certification-signature, or equivalent signing field without explicit authorization to sign that specific application. A normal cover-letter closing does not grant signing permission. The [EmailConfirmationChecker](../email-confirmation-checker/SKILL.md) checks a matching email receipt after submission.
