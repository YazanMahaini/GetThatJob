# Reusable application profile (private)

This file holds verified form answers that are useful across applications and are not already clear in the approved CV. Update it after each checked application draft. Keep the exact source and date so future applications can reuse stable facts without asking again. Never put this file in the plugin repository or upload it to an employer.

## Reusable personal facts

| Field | Verified answer | Source and confirmation date | Reuse scope or recheck rule | Last used |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Examples of fields to record when confirmed: date and place of birth, nationality, residence, preferred application contact, language proficiency, availability, and other recurring form answers absent from the CV. Leave unknown fields out; do not enter placeholders as answers.

## Portal or country-specific answers

| Exact question or field | Verified answer | Source and confirmation date | Portal, country, or application scope | Recheck rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record date-entry conventions and eligibility answers here only with their scope. A work-authorization answer for one country, a referee-contact decision, a salary expectation, a disclosure, or a consent answer must not be treated as a universal answer for another application.

## Corrections and unresolved conflicts

Record a correction with its date and source. If sources disagree, mark the field unresolved and ask before reuse; do not silently keep the stronger claim.
